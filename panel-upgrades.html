An Honest Panel Recommendation

A panel replacement is a major investment. SoLit Electric inspects the condition, capacity, and safety of the equipment before recommending one. If a code-compliant repair is the right answer, that is the answer you will get.

When Is a Panel Upgrade Needed?

These conditions deserve a professional evaluation. They do not automatically mean every component must be replaced.

  • Known hazardous equipment: Federal Pacific and Zinsco panels can present serious safety and insurance concerns.
  • Not enough capacity: A new HVAC system, generator, electric range, or EV charger may require additional amperage or circuit space.
  • Recurring electrical symptoms: Frequent breaker trips, heat, buzzing, corrosion, or flickering lights can signal a damaged connection or an overloaded system.
  • Insurance or code requirements: Older equipment may need repair or replacement before a policy is issued or renovation work is approved.
