When Do You Actually Need a Panel Upgrade?

  • You have an outdated brand: Federal Pacific (FPE) or Zinsco panels are known fire hazards and often fail to trip during overloads.
  • You're running out of space: You are adding a new HVAC system, a whole-home generator, or an EV charging station and need more amperage.
  • Flickering lights and tripped breakers: If you are constantly resetting breakers when you run the microwave and the vacuum at the same time, your home has outgrown its current electrical capacity.
  • Insurance requirements: Many homeowners insurance companies in Louisiana will drop coverage if your panel is over a certain age or from a dangerous manufacturer.
