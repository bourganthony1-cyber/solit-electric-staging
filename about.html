No Job Too Small. No Corporate Upselling.

SoLit Electric wasn't built in a corporate boardroom. It was built right here in Louisiana by Yancie David, on the simple idea that honesty is the best business model.

We believe that when you call an electrician, you should get a straight answer. We don't believe in high-pressure sales tactics. If a job just requires a simple, inexpensive fix, that's exactly what we'll tell you. We treat our customers like our neighbors—because that's exactly what you are.

From swapping out a hard-to-reach light fixture to completely upgrading a home's electrical panel or wiring in a new generator, we approach every single job with the exact same level of respect, transparency, and hard work.
